## Drafting rules for AI

- **Never invent** memories, names, places, numbers, or client details. Use only owner-verified facts. Mark anything missing as a gap for the owner to fill.
- **Never manufacture** typos, grammar errors, or fake sloppiness. Voice texture yes, artificial imperfection no.
- **Never inflate** credentials or results beyond what `.work.biz/strategy/` and the proof files support.
- **Owner pass:** <required before publish? for which formats?> An AI draft is a draft. State plainly whether a human pass is mandatory and how long it takes.

## Detector note

AI detectors reward imperfection and penalize polish. This profile optimizes for sounding like the owner **to human readers**. Do not degrade professional credibility to chase a detector score, because buyers and recruiters read the same text. If a detector number matters for a given surface, the owner pass is what moves it, not stylistic sabotage.
