# Voice Retro — what the draft said vs what the owner changed

**Purpose:** make content skill measurable instead of felt. Every published asset gets one short entry. The value is not the individual entry, it is the pattern across ten of them: the edits you keep making are your voice telling us what the drafting rules are still getting wrong, and they should be folded back into `reference/VOICE_PROFILE.md` as they repeat.

**How to use it.** After the owner voice pass, before publishing, record what changed. Keep it to the edits that matter. A reworded sentence counts; a comma does not. Then add the numbers when they land.

**The signal to watch:** owner edits per piece should *fall* over time, not because the owner stops caring but because the drafts start arriving closer to the voice. If it does not fall after five or six pieces, the drafting rules are wrong and `VOICE_PROFILE.md` needs amending, not the owner working harder.

**Amendment rule:** when the same class of edit appears three times, amend `VOICE_PROFILE.md`. Record the amendment in its revision header.
